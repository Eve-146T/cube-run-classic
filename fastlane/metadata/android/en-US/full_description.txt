Cube Run Classic is an endless runner. The world rushes toward you down a neon three-lane track, and your cube has to dodge, jump, slide and slam its way through everything in the way.

Features:

* swipe to switch lanes, jump, slide and slam.
* An endless, procedurally generated track
* A relentless difficulty ramp that rewards nerve and near-misses.
* Tight, tactile feedback with synchronised sound and haptics.
